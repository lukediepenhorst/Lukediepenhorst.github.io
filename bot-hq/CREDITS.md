# Credits

Everything in this office is drawn in code. There are no downloaded models, textures, or audio files.

- Geometry, materials, and the logo are procedural (generated when the page loads).
- Ambience is filtered noise from the Web Audio API. The background notes are a short pentatonic pattern played by an oscillator. Both start only after a tap, and the mute choice is stored on this device.
- The typefaces Outfit and IBM Plex Mono are loaded from Google Fonts and used under the SIL Open Font License.
